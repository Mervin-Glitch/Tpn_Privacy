Privacy Policy

Twelve Past Nowhere  •  Effective [July 21, 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how Twelve Past Nowhere ("we," "us," "our") handles information in connection with our mobile games, including Cipher Notebook and Ferry Master (each an "App," collectively the "Apps"). We may release additional Apps in the future, and this Policy will apply to those as well unless a separate policy is posted for a specific App.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to play. There is no username, password, email address, or profile associated with your use of the Apps.

Your game progress (levels completed, settings, and similar data) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive, transmit, or store this progress data on any server we control.

Information Collected Automatically

If an App includes advertising or other third-party services, those services may automatically collect certain information from your device in order to function — for example:

  • Device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID)
  • General, non-precise location information (such as country or region, typically derived from IP address)
  • App interaction data related to ads (such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)
  • Basic technical information (device type, operating system version, app version) for crash reporting and stability purposes, if such a service is enabled

This information is collected and processed by the third-party services listed below, not by us directly, though it occurs through your use of our Apps and we are disclosing it here for transparency.

Third-Party Services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Services (app distribution, licensing) — policies.google.com/privacy

How Information Is Used

Where applicable, the automatically collected information described above is used to:

  • Display and measure the performance of in-app advertising, including rewarded ads used for hints
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues to improve app stability
  • Understand aggregate, anonymized usage patterns to improve the Apps

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use any collected information to build an individual profile of you beyond what the third-party services above use for their own advertising or analytics purposes.

Data Storage & Retention

Since we do not operate user accounts, we do not retain personal data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party services (such as AdMob) is retained according to those providers' own retention policies, which are outside our direct control.

You can remove all locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below so we can address it.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android devices), which limits — though may not entirely eliminate — ad personalization.
  • If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights regarding information collected by third-party services, which can generally be exercised directly with those providers, or by contacting us and we will assist where we are able.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled in connection with our Apps, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as new features or services are added to our Apps. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
